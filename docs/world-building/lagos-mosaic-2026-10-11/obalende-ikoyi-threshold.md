# Obalende / Ikoyi threshold: Eleshin Street at Keffi and Ojo

October 11, 2026. One researched street pocket and conditional fictional-world brief, not a second citywide survey. Documentation only; coordinator review pending.

## Scope and preservation

We select **Eleshin Street between Keffi Street and Ojo Street**, with the adjacent Odo Street return and Igbo Street branch. This gives the island mosaic an intermediate layer of home, work, education-related errands and everyday services. It is not a boundary survey separating Obalende from Ikoyi, not a claim that all Obalende is informal, and not a poor-to-rich transition strip. The source road network connects to Obalende Road to the north and Awolowo Road through Keffi to the south; it supplies a usable research interface without converting military grounds, schools or private compounds into shortcuts.

Read during this pass: root `AGENTS.md`; coordinator-owned `README.md` in this folder, including the second-round allocation; the previous [Old Lagos Island dossier](old-lagos-island.md); and the Victoria Island endpoint passages of [island-and-coast.md](island-and-coast.md). Existing dossiers, README and integration plan remain read-only. The prior palace, religious-space and household-privacy evidence is preserved: nothing here authorizes a new palace entrance, sacred interior or Old Island household shortcut.

We provide one pocket as explicitly assigned, rather than the first-round template's two slices. No final engine placement, overall map envelope, canonical district ID, theatre relocation, geographic-to-world registration or height datum is supplied. The two existing Old Island walks remain their original reference slices; this pocket is additional research, not their replacement.

Evidence labels:

- **F: source fact.** A cited institution, business or local report explicitly states it. Addresses and published programmes do not prove facade condition or everyday conduct at every nearby doorway.
- **M: topology inference.** Fresh OSM shared-node membership or ordered geometry supports a connection. This is not a survey of accessible pavements, crossing safety, tenancy or current appearance.
- **P: authored proposal.** Fictional premises, household arrangements, route lengths, clearances, activities, materials, sound, weather and the event. Design values are not measurements of Lagos.
- **U: unknown.** Not established, inaccessible, contradictory or outside this pocket's evidence.
