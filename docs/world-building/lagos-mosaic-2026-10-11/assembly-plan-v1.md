# Lagos mosaic assembly plan

October 11, 2026. Coordinator design proposal based on the six first-round dossiers. This is a composition and asset handoff, not implemented scene data. The table IDs below are document-local references, not registered district IDs.

## Build neighborhoods, not five theme parks

The twelve reference slices supply distinct local street graphs. Their 230-270 metre showcase walks are not their entire neighborhoods: preserve the additional side-street depth, full return circuits and ordinary residential intervals described in each dossier. An optional shop or courtyard excursion must not inflate the length of the public through-route.

The earlier five biomes remain scene-reading anchors: transport, coast, lagoon, study/work and freight. They do not define every building, household, occupation or sound inside a district. Do not apply a biome-wide poverty, rust, luxury, graffiti or crowd preset.

The previous 6 by 4.5 kilometre envelope is withdrawn as a constraint. Assemble local circuits and necessary crossings before choosing the final envelope. Shorten selected intervening journeys; retain human-scale rooms, vehicles, clearances and frontage depth. Do not reduce movement speed to manufacture distance.

## How a slice occupies space

Design targets in this section are initial authoring values, not local measurements or accepted performance numbers.

Treat each slice as a main public route, branches and destinations, not a straight 250 m set. Preserve the mapped junction order where used; record every fictional extra return lane separately. M01's authored return lane must not be represented as a named OSM street, and M08's longer loop must not be squeezed into its short showcase walk.

For each main-route segment, specify frontage on both sides, entrances, circulation, road/drain edges, visible next junction, optional destinations and public/private limits. A service yard needs an independent delivery position; a residence needs an actual private threshold. Keep deeper dwellings and commercial storage beyond the first facade row where players can see into the block.

Begin with five to eight individually authored frontage groups per showcase walk. This is a composition target, not a requirement to fill every metre with commerce. Groups may contain several doors, shared stairs, a courtyard or a quieter wall interval. A usable branch should reveal the next corner and a reason to follow it rather than end immediately behind the first building.

Protect continuous pedestrian circulation using the dimensions proposed in each dossier; do not silently widen all researched streets into identical avenues. Where the authored accessible route differs from unverified real geometry, retain that distinction in production metadata. Essential destinations have a ground-level interaction option; private homes are never mandatory shortcuts.

## Developer asset assembly checklist

This consolidates requirements; it does not create or register assets. Slice-specific requirements remain in the six dossiers.

### Meshes and architecture

- Keep a shared structural library for walls, floors, roof edges, stairs, drainage crossings and doors, with separate authored frontage assemblies for each street.
- Build recognizable household layouts: multiple entries around a shared yard, a private compound behind its threshold, upper residence above a shop, and a shop with distinct work/storage/customer space.
- Maintain separate terminal, campus-approach, working-waterfront, landing and industrial-edge assemblies. A public foreground does not imply access to every adjacent facility.
- Supply narrow/deep and wider/setback frontages, corners, recesses and longer roof runs. Variation comes from layout and use, not randomly scattering identical boxes.
- Give moving or usable objects interaction geometry: shop shutters, handcarts, collection shelves, seats, canoe berths and vehicle service bays. Static dressing must not be labeled working gameplay.

### Shaders and textures

- Author surface condition from exposure and maintenance masks: roof edges, repaired render, water-contact bands, sheltered paint and vehicle exhaust near actual emitters.
- Keep material identity separate from weather state and district identity. Wetness does not make every street muddy; industrial location does not require every steel surface to be rusted.
- Treat current facade/roof palettes as pending where only map tags or textual references exist. Historical photographed conditions need the original place/date recorded.
- Store drainage wetness, puddle appearance and water-contact effects against the same authored water-height reference used by collision and navigation.
- Keep real logos, performances, campaign posters and culturally specific text out of the shared decal library until evidence and reuse permissions are resolved.

### Dynamic systems and spawners

- Assign trips between homes, workplaces, public services, shops and transit points. Occupation and current activity are separate; the same NPC can work, travel, rest and decline interaction.
- Give every destination arrival, waiting, interaction and departure positions outside protected circulation. Local demand drives queues; district identity alone does not.
- Carry traffic along reviewed road graphs and vehicle swept paths. Hawker movement and boarding require eligible stopping/gap conditions and safe retreat positions; random lane spawns are insufficient.
- Separate public pedestrian, vehicle, invited courtyard, controlled facility and water graphs. Distinguish visual proximity, mapped graph connectivity and permission to traverse.
- Attach audio to functioning sources and time states. Shops, generators and moving vehicles must not emit the same loop continuously throughout a whole district.
- Use connected runoff/drainage domains with local level references. Geographic source nodes contain no certified water or ground elevation.

## Event variety and neighborhood continuity

Retain the first-round loading/repair events as a shared minor-incident family, not the defining encounter in all twelve slices. Initial design target: no more than one navigation-obstructing member of that family active in a player's immediate neighborhood circuit at a time. Avoid another obstruction directly on its alternate route.

The second round must add events involving social timing, temporary public activity, changing destinations or altered movement demand rather than repeatedly spawning a broken vehicle. These are fictional event proposals; specific cultural performances require research and review before use.

An event needs a cause, spatial footprint, affected actors, duration/clearing condition, alternate public path and an interaction that can be declined. A quieter destination may become active without physically blocking the player. Do not make negotiation aggressive by default or encode a neighborhood's identity as conflict.
