# Port and working streets: two Lagos reference slices

Research and authorship date: October 11, 2026. All external sources listed below were accessed on October 11, 2026. Documentation only; no engine integration or placement approval.

We propose two bounded neighborhood experiences: **Liverpool Road at Idunganran Road, Apapa**, and **Gaskiya College Road between the unnamed residential branches at nodes 1047196529 and 1047195971, in the Ijora working-street reference area**. These are street samples, not descriptions of all Apapa, Ijora or Orile. The second slice does not claim an Orile administrative boundary or relocate Orile's markets into Ijora.

## Evidence and composition contract

- **[F] Source-confirmed:** a named source explicitly reports something, with its date and limits retained. An institutional claim is not an independent survey.
- **[M] Map-supported:** OSM names, tags, node coordinates and shared-node topology; interpretation beyond those fields is identified as inference. Mapping does not establish current public access, buildings, materials or conditions.
- **[P] Authored proposal:** fictional buildings, businesses, routines, route changes, dimensions, sounds and composite connectors. These are design choices, not observed Lagos conditions.

All walking sequences, shop inventories, facade forms, socioeconomic contrasts and incident logic below are **[P]**, unless individually marked otherwise. No real business name is invented or assigned to an unverified frontage. No building age, elevation, measured road width, household income, religious affiliation or ethnic composition is established here.

We compress travel between slices, not the scale of people, vehicles, rooms or courtyards. There is no assumed 6 by 4.5 km world envelope. These proposals neither move nor relabel canonical districts, theatre placement, maps, destinations or IDs. The coordinator owns eventual composition and resolves joins with other dossiers.

Geographic evidence is WGS84 decimal degrees in **[longitude east, latitude north]** order, with no altitude. OSM API bounding boxes use **west longitude, south latitude, east longitude, north latitude**; XML nodes themselves provide named `lat` and `lon` attributes. Geographic degrees are not game metres. The shared source convention remains metres **x east, y up, z south**, with **UE(X,Y,Z) = 100 * source(z,x,y)**, applied once. No projection, world origin, vertical datum, rotation, translation or final engine positions are assigned. Earlier sector documents' research frames are not imported as this dossier's coordinate convention.
