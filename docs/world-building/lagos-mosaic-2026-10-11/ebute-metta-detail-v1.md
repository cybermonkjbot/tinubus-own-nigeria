## Room And Interaction Blueprint

Each premise has three rotated room volumes. Household examples use a 1.6 by 3.2 m threshold, 3.6 by 5.4 m living volume and 3 by 5.2 m service volume. Repair profiles use a 1.8 by 3.2 m collection volume, separate work volume and separate stock/cure volume. Their dimensioned bounds are stored in the packet, not inferred from imagery. Walls, corridor links and exit geometry still need authoring; gaps between volumes are not automatically navigable passages.

Each room has a 1.2 m clear-width, 2.4 m clear-height doorway target. Private rooms do not connect to public navigation. Staff-only tool rooms, invitation-controlled fitting spaces and resident-only rooms retain distinct states. Door targets must be reconciled with a real floor/corridor mesh and clearance review before entry is enabled.

Service queues use a separate 1.8 by 5.4 m proposed property recess in the unused frontage bay, not sockets placed through the counter. Up to two waiting sockets sit 2.7 m apart. A called customer moves to a separate approach socket in front of a 0.6 m deep, 1.4 m wide counter; movement waits for actual clearance, not an artificial timer. Overflow means defer service or leave, never grow onto the proposed 2 m clear public footway. Tool, customer and waiting reservations release on cancellation. The packet carries world coordinates for every room, prop, doorway, queue and emitter.

No stairs or upper household interior is invented inside the inherited shell. Existing upper facade pieces remain exterior source components; any later upper-floor route requires its own replacement floor, structural clearance and step-free alternative.

## Developer Asset Checklist

### Meshes And Architecture

- Replace shallow shells with parcel-specific room depths, floors, ceilings, real openings, circulation and exits. Do not append unseen rooms to a collision-solid shell.
- Author 25 dimensioned mesh recipes: counter, bench, chair frame, foam offcuts, fabric rack, tool board, sewing table, fitting screen, garment rail, stock bin, provisions shelf, wash basin, stool, covered container, folding table, drying rail, clamps, wrapped collection item, job board, curtain, shutter, drain cover, canopy, wall light and fan.
- Use center pivots with explicit half-height placement offsets. Closed-box or convex collision applies to substantial objects; scraps, signs and curtain cloth have no independent solid collision. Privacy needs a door or boundary, not cloth collision alone.
- Starting triangle targets are 2,400/900/220 at near/12 m/35 m LOD tiers, not generated counts or performance results. Tiny tool detail becomes normals beyond 12 m; doorway and canopy silhouettes remain. Nanite is not a prerequisite.
- Shell, doorway, canopy, floor and drain collision need joint review. The recipe catalog includes canopy/drain/light assets but does not falsely report them placed or generated.

### Materials And Textures

- Mineral render uses separate substrate, retained paint, bounded repair, shelter and water-contact masks. Authored roughness target is 0.72-0.92, with wet roughness no lower than 0.48 and bounded darkening of 0.18. No universal flood stripe.
- Painted metal is dielectric until substrate is exposed. Exposed metal is metallic; oxide is not. Fastener streaks follow actual roof/canopy orientation. No rust shader on concrete, timber or fabric.
- Timber grain follows boards, with distinct end grain; fresh sanding and adhesive marks attach to the active repaired joint. A work-state change must not randomize the entire item.
- Foam/fabric uses pore or weave, folds, compression and separate seam UVs. No named cultural textile, campaign image, religious motif or branded decal without reviewed references and reuse clearance.
- Washed plastic separates stock equipment from cleaning equipment. Scuffs follow contact masks; water films do not imply contaminated goods.

### Systems And Spawners

- Admit workers only at reviewed work sockets, one active worker per tool. Household fronts have no automatic workers or vendors. Customer caps derive from admitted counters, not a citywide crowd multiplier.
- Preserve repair item IDs across receive, assessment, work, curing, inspection and collection. Cancelled work retains its last completed stage; finished objects do not spawn from an unfinished animation.
- Work-state pacing targets include 15-40 seconds to assess a joint, 30-75 seconds to glue/clamp and 300-900 seconds to cure. Sewing targets 40-100 seconds; packing 10-25 seconds. These are simulation timings, not measured trade durations; the player can leave rather than wait for a job.
- Shared rooms reserve a time slot, set out, work, pack away and restore domestic use. Occupation and language are not assigned by gender, ethnicity or income cues.
- Candidate dialogue includes `How far?`, `Abeg, give me small space.` and `No wahala.` with a 45-second authored bark cooldown. These are unrecorded fictional lines pending local performance review, not verified utterances from these streets. English/Pidgin choice is individual, not compulsory.
- Do not spawn Danfo, Keke, Okada or hawkers from room blueprints. Local vehicle access, stopping geometry and current restrictions remain separate unresolved work.

## Audio, Light And Weather

Eighteen positioned activity emitters sit inside their respective rooms at 1.2 m height. Authored reach is 7-13 m with a smooth falloff, not a hard sphere audible at its edge. Sewing pulses, tool taps, fabric movement and packaging sounds are gated to active work states. Curing and closed workrooms can be quiet. Proposed closed-door occlusion is -14 dB with a 1.2 kHz low-pass target, requiring actual wall traces and door portals. These are mix targets, not measured Lagos sound levels. No audio files or recording rights are present.

Speech and tools use separate buses. No rail, prayer, popular music or generator layer is borrowed as automatic neighborhood identity. A generator requires a verified ventilated parcel, exhaust direction and cleared source; none is added here.

Rain targets a 60-second wetness rise and 900-second drying response, driven by local exposure masks. Canopy impact sound differs from sheltered-room sound. Clothing and queues relocate only to admitted free property recesses. Puddles are bounded authoring masks; unknown grades cannot support flood depths, a catchment model or dynamic water-plane elevation. Deep flooding is disabled in this pocket.

The hazy variant targets 1.6 times the baseline volumetric extinction and 0.85 times sun saturation, retaining shaded-wall/sky differences rather than applying a global orange filter. These are artistic controls, not meteorological measurements. Evening routes stay readable through public lighting; individual domestic light schedules neither create a universal outage nor determine whether the player can navigate.
