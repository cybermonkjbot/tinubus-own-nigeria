# Old Lagos Island: two street-scale slices

Research and authoring date: October 11, 2026. Documentation only. Owner: Old Lagos Island assignment. Read with the coordinator-owned [shared contract](README.md), which was read during this pass and is unchanged.

## Scope and evidence rules

We propose two complementary slices: **Iga Idunganran Street / Oroyinyin Street**, with residential thresholds and a small public-street circuit; and **Breadfruit Street / Balogun Street**, with trade, repair, storage and domestic life behind commercial frontage. These are selected street portions, not definitions of Isale Eko, Idumota, Oke Arin or Balogun's boundaries. The second slice is specifically the Breadfruit/Balogun interface, not a surveyed Oke Arin market precinct. No defensible Oke Arin market boundary was established in this pass.

Evidence labels apply throughout:

- **F: source-confirmed statement.** The cited source actually makes this statement. An undated community account confirms what that account says, not independent verification of every historical assertion or today's operating conditions.
- **M: map-supported inference.** Fresh OSM XML supplies named ways, tags and shared-node topology. This is volunteered mapping, not a pedestrian-access survey, measurement of street width or parcel-use inventory.
- **P: authored game proposal.** Fictional compression, layout, activities, building envelopes, dimensions, events and tuning. These are not claims about real occupants or extant buildings.
- **U: unresolved.** Evidence is absent, contradictory, inaccessible, old or insufficient for the proposed claim.

All interior descriptions, material palettes, NPC routines and dimensions below are **P** unless explicitly marked otherwise. Source-backed cultural context does not turn a proposed shop or courtyard into a documented address. No real business name is invented. Unnamed fictional premises require fictional-location metadata; real street names remain geographic reference labels, not certification of an exact recreation.

We compress journeys between slices, not people, doorways, vehicles or courtyard space. We set no overall world envelope, engine origin, theatre placement, canonical district ID or final engine position. Existing canonical content, maps and destinations remain unchanged. This dossier is not registered renderer data and triggers no renderer packaging or mobile changes.
