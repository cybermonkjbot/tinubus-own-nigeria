# Shared secondary art-pass contract

October 11, 2026. Authored production directions, applied with each sector manifest.

## Scope and ownership

Preserve all primary roads, major buildings, terrain, water geometry, IDs, public/private boundaries, collision, navigation and gameplay systems. Junction IDs are review anchors, not world placements. Attach to an approved existing host in local coordinates; leave world transform unset until primary registration is accepted. Missing hosts defer placement, not the rest of a manifest.

Shared source features receive one detail owner. Reference shared hosts across sectors rather than duplicate stains, cables, litter, lights or emitters. Existing secondary asset kits may be reused after manifest compatibility review; this document neither creates assets nor imports them.

## Vertex/PBR recipes

- Laterite: the requested dust is a deposit, not replacement terrain. Concentrate it within the lower 0.5 m of local wall contact, unevenly feathered and interrupted by swept entrances and rain washing. Tires receive tread-recess/lower-sidewall dust; supplied foliage receives restrained low, road-facing deposits. Never apply one red tint to whole buildings, wheels or canopies.
- Humidity: green growth and dark mold-like staining need persistent damp, shade and a receiving surface. Use joints, seepage and actual drainage contact; retain dry/maintained patches. Do not assume every channel is concrete, wet, polluted or refuse-filled.
- Power: dense matte soot follows a supplied exhaust's direction and actual receiving wall. Oily deposits remain distinct and localized to service contact. Off-state soot may persist; off-state heat distortion may not.
- Rust: severe oxide is an option for selected older corrugated sheets, gate edges and fasteners, beside intact/repaired examples. Preserve roof silhouette, metal profile, gate swing and all supports. Rust/coating are nonmetal layers over sound metal, not fully metallic orange paint.
- Contact: polished handles, threshold centres, tire contact, cart rubs and worn timber follow real touch paths. Texture wear must not create implied structural collapse, new collision holes or slippery gameplay states.

Suggested authoring masks, only if unused by the host contract: R dust, G persistent damp/algae, B substrate-specific deterioration, A contact grime. Keep transient wetness and soot separate. Never repurpose existing vertex channels silently. Use texture masks for narrow streaks and print; broad vertex fields should not require subdividing primary architecture.

## Decals and lettering

Stage original fictional campaign paper, hand-painted use-matched shop text, overlapping church artwork, obituary remnants and the requested `POST NO BILL` or `THIS HOUSE IS NOT FOR SALE - BEWARE OF 419` only on appropriate approved hosts. Real identities and branded packaging remain in the deferred register until approved. Do not infer current signs, deaths, affiliation or business use from historic source photos or map tags.

Layer old paste → faded torn sheet → sparse newer patch. Retain one readable hero surface per cluster, blank intervals and maintained paint. Receiver bounds must exclude openings, glass unless intended, people, traffic signs and neighbouring surfaces. Campaign scenario dates and artwork identities belong in production metadata; no invented current campaign is presented as observed.

## Scatter and makeshift infrastructure

Initial per-cluster recipes: 3–7 crushed clear water sachets, 1–3 sausage-roll wrappers and 1–2 collapsed/tied black nylon bags in an approved catchment, not on every frontage. Gala branding remains conditional. Use small reflective folds and opaque/low-overdraw distant substitutes where appropriate; exact rendering choices remain integration work.

Roof stones, tire seating/barricades and gutter planks dress only approved existing arrangements. Stones need approved bearing/contact. Tires cannot create new traffic control. Planks cannot add a crossing or change walkable collision. Keep doors, thresholds, emergency space, vehicle swept volumes and reserved work/loading areas clear. Clean and active spaces are not clutter targets by default.

Cable splines follow supplied poles and building fittings: uneven sag, parallel diameters, restrained tied bundles and slack service loops within the primary clearance envelope. No new poles, energization, climbing routes or illegal connections. A tapped branch requires a specific primary-approved story/utility state; visual disorder does not establish illegality.

## Micro-VFX recipes

| Effect | Host/state | Receiver and shutdown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flies | Existing exposed organic waste | Tight intermittent orbit; remove/cull when host is covered, absent or distant. Sachets alone are insufficient |
| AC condensation | Operating supplied window AC with actual outlet | Drops and small impacts on a valid surface; stop with primary operating state; no invented pooled-water volume |
| Heat distortion | Approved hot asphalt or running generator | Soft bounded refraction, away from text/interaction targets; cool/off or unsuitable rain state disables it |
| Rain drips | Primary rain/residual runoff and an actual edge | Follow supplied low points and receiver; no flow through decks, roofs or walls; stop on state expiry |

These are Niagara briefs, not new components or systems. Consume existing primary state. Prefer bounded/shared emitters and scalability culling; exact counts, distances, audio and performance require separate integration and acceptance. No emitted effect changes navigation, inventory, power or weather authority.

## Handoff and acceptance record

Record detail ID, host/revision, slot/local transform, evidence class, artwork approval, deterministic seed, mask mapping, receiver bounds, route/utility exclusions, primary state dependency, LOD/cull policy, owner and acceptance status.

Source review checks anchor names/IDs, host conditions, clearances and primary-event compatibility. Later rendered review compares matched player-height day/night and dry/wet views, contact, silhouettes, text and effect receivers. Document review is not visual acceptance. No tests, builds, packaging, imports, deployment or new preview processes are authorized by this document.
