# Nigerian open-world game: remaining team backlog

October 11, 2026. Scope: the standalone Unreal translation of our existing Companion game, including the approved Lagos and rural-expedition direction. This is not the backlog for the entire Baddie social app.

We are building a connected Nigerian open-world game around our existing accounts, saved Companions, Homes and game rules. The GTA reference describes the intended sense of freedom, city life and interaction; it does not replace our planned feature inventory or authorize copied maps, characters or assets.

## How to assign and close these goals

Owner labels below are suggested team responsibilities, not existing employee assignments. Assign a named accountable owner and dependencies to each ID. Several goals can proceed in parallel; especially, world/content production does not need to wait for the account-transport work.

Statuses describe the Unreal gap, not whether the feature already works in Baddie's mobile/web game. “Source exists” means reuse and finish it, not rebuild it. A goal is not closed by a completed agent turn, a codec, asset count, saved editor map or source-only review.

For every goal, record separately: source implementation, source review, Windows integration, native assets, compilation, in-game verification, target performance and release. Preserve existing behavior unless we explicitly approve a scope change. No percentage-complete estimate is supported by these records.

## Decisions to keep separate from approved implementation

1. **Expansion geography:** select the factual/additive versus fictional-separated-island composition and approve its topology before building the expansion terrain. Preserve the existing fictional metric city's saved identities in either case.
2. **Customisation/toy shop checkout:** current source exposes owned-item use/preview but says purchases are unavailable. Decide the intended checkout/policy before calling that a working feature to port. This does not block the distinct game-economy/POS work.
3. **Loans and real-money conversion:** the source explicitly leaves lending rules, issuance, repayment and conversion unconfigured/disabled. Do not treat draft fields as approved loan or cash-conversion gameplay.
4. **Retired modes and new wishlists:** Room toys is retired from current room-game starts. Property trading/mortgages, a full romance system, arbitrary careers/sports and unlimited playable boat modes are not established by the inspected plan. Do not silently add or reactivate them because GTA has analogous systems.
5. **Performance and platform sign-off:** agree measured target-device budgets and release gates. The rural brief's proposed performance target and software preview cannot establish a shipping result. Browser/GPU streaming, console release and new paid infrastructure are not part of the inspected first Windows slice.
